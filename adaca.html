ABOUT ADCA

What is ADCA?

💻

Advanced Diploma in Computer Application

ADCA is a comprehensive computer application course designed to develop practical knowledge of computers and commonly used digital tools.

During the course, students can learn computer fundamentals, operating systems, MS Office, internet applications, accounting software, programming concepts and web-related skills.
